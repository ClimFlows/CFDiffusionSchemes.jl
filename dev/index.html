CFDiffusionSchemes.ThermoFluxConsistent — Type
ThermoFluxConsistent(; k_T, k_q)

Thermodynamically consistent fluxes of consvar and q - heat flux down the temperature gradient, with thermal diffusivity k_T - composition flux down the gradient of q, corrected for barodiffusion, with diffusivity k_q

Downgradient fluxes of consvar and q with diffusivities [m² s⁻¹] k_T for consvar and k_q for q.

CFDiffusionSchemes.ViscosityScheme — Type
ViscosityScheme(; dyn_visc)

Newtonian viscosity with a constant dynamic viscosity μ = dyn_visc [Pa s] and the Stokes hypothesis (zero bulk viscosity): the stress is τ = 2μ (S - (∇·u) I / 3), with S the strain rate and I the identity tensor.

CFDiffusionSchemes.consvar_flux — Method
flux = consvar_flux(scheme, ρ, Cp, ∂T, ∂cons, qflux, conj, ∂consvar_∂q)

Diffusive flux of consvar, along the direction of the gradients. ThermoFluxConsistent carries the heat flux -ρ Cp kT ∂T [W m⁻²] (density ρ [kg m⁻³], heat capacity Cp [J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹], temperature gradient ∂T [K m⁻¹]), converted by `heattoconsvar(argumentsqflux,conj,∂consvar∂q);ThermoFluxDowngradient` goes down the gradient of consvar, -ρ k_T ∂cons, and ignores the other arguments.

CFDiffusionSchemes.heat_to_consvar — Method
flux = heat_to_consvar(scheme, JT, qflux, conj, ∂consvar_∂q)

Flux of consvar carrying the heat flux JT [W m⁻²] (the reduced heat flux): JT / conj, with conj the conjugate variable of consvar, plus ∂consvar_∂q * qflux for ThermoFluxConsistent, where qflux is the flux of composition and ∂consvar_∂q the derivative of consvar with respect to q at constant pressure and temperature. The energy delivered by the flux of consvar and the flux of composition is then conj * flux + chempot * qflux, with chempot the modified chemical potential. ThermoFluxDowngradient gives JT / conj.
